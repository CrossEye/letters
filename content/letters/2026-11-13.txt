Title: The Poor Dear

Jeff Maguire, the poor dear, is being bullied.

I guess the mediators hurt his feelings when they told him that he's not in
charge of our school, that the people we elected to run it and the people
they've hired are in charge. Poor Jeff. He really wants to threaten to take his
toys and go home. But they're not his toys. Not even the gymnasium, which he's
trying to take over. All because the school has the temerity to favor
children's basketball programs over Scott Person's pickleball time.

So after being rebuked by Connecticut officials, after being told that the
school not only does not need to account to him, and that he can't condition on
anything the transfer of voter-approved funds, after being informed that the
school has the legal right to demand its whole budget up front, he attempts
another end-run around the voters and the law. Last night the BOS passed a
motion that would put all school funds in an account "under the supervision and
oversight of the Town Treasurer."

Anne Cremé abstained, saying that this is not the business of the BOS. It's
hard to blame Dave Gostanian and Christine Randazzo for voting in favor of this
blatantly illegal law. They're too new to realize that they're just pawns in
Maguire's petty attempts to control something that's not his to control. But
Maguire and Person know what they're doing. They know they don't have the
authority. Maguire was informed of it by the state.

He just thinks that if he cries loudly enough, people will let him have his
cookie just to shut him up.

The poor dear.
