Title: Weir is a Nice Guy; That's not Enough
Date: 2026-08-21
Topics: Steve Weir, 55th District
TeaserParagraphs: 3
Content:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Recent letters have praised Steve Weir as a kind, accessible, hard-working man.
I believe every word. If we were choosing a neighbor of the year, he would get
my vote.

But we are choosing a legislator, and a legislator's job is not proclamations
and ribbon cuttings. It is votes. His admirers point to his perfect attendance.
Perfect attendance is fine, but only if those votes support our community.
Consider what four years of showing up has produced.

This May the House passed the budget adjustment, SB 1, by 127-21. It carries
roughly $630,000 in new aid for the five towns of the 55th: about $98,000 for
Andover, $270,000 for Hebron, $149,000 for Marlborough, plus more for Bolton and
eastern Glastonbury. Of the 49 Republicans voting, 28 said yes. Weir voted no,
on money for his own towns.

After the Concierge Apartments disaster in Rocky Hill, where an out-of-state
owner let a complex rot until thousands of tenants had to be relocated and the
Attorney General had to extract over $5 million, the legislature answered with
SB 274. It passed the Senate and cleared committee unanimously. On the session's
last night, Weir and a few allies threatened to run out the clock, and the bill
died without a vote. Weir sits on the board of the Connecticut Apartment
Association, whose president testified against the bill. Whose side was he on
that night?

The pattern runs deeper. He voted no on requiring safety fences around swimming
pools; it passed 102-45. He voted no on ending child marriage in Connecticut; it
passed 98-45. He was one of five no votes on approving the State Police union
contract, one of four against giving small businesses a single point of contact
in state government, one of three against reappointing Judge Elizabeth Bozzuto,
and the lone Republican no on enforcing our boating laws.

This is not moderate versus conservative. When a bill passes 143-5 and your
representative is one of the five, again and again, that is not independence. It
is reflex.

A thousand ribbon cuttings do not make up for a no vote on your own towns' aid.
A hundred proclamations will not shelter a tenant or fence a pool. Steve Weir is
a nice guy. That is not enough.
