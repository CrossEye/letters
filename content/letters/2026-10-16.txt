I went to the Oct. 7 candidates' forum in Glastonbury expecting a town hall. It
wasn't one; eight candidates spoke, including Ellen Paul and Steve Weir, but the
audience didn't get to ask questions. It was a good event anyway, and I'm
grateful to its organizers. But I came with questions for Rep. Weir, so I'll put
them to him here.

1. What is the bill you're proudest of? Not constituent help, which everyone
agrees you do well, and not one you cosponsored alongside dozens of colleagues,
but a bill you wrote and shepherded through yourself, or one you fought hard to
defeat.

2. Your campaign website offers your biography, your community service, and
principles like "Protect taxpayers." It doesn't name a single bill, position, or
plan. What are two specific things you'll do next term, and how will you get
them passed?

3. In 1,351 roll calls over four years, I count 95 times you voted to the right
of your own caucus, but only three times you crossed party lines on a vote where
the parties actually divided. Did I miss any crossovers?

4. Senate Bill 274 was written after the Concierge Apartments disaster in Rocky
Hill, where an out-of-state owner let a complex decay until thousands of tenants
had to be relocated. The bill raised penalties for repeat fire and building-code
violations and required out-of-state owners to register with the towns where
their buildings sit. It passed the Senate and cleared committee without a single
no vote. On the session's last night, you and a few allies threatened to run out
the clock, and it died without a vote. Why that bill? And if you opposed it, why
not simply vote no?

5. In May you voted against a budget adjustment carrying about $630,000 in new
aid to our five towns, a bill that 27 of the 48 Republicans voting supported.
What would you tell taxpayers about that vote?

There is one more question I would have liked to ask, but this page keeps
letters to state and local matters. I sent it to Rep. Weir by email on Sept. 10
and have not had a reply. Any answer he gives will be published in full at
andoverct.info/reports/ahm/disavowal.

Ellen Paul answers questions like these on her website. I'd like to hear Rep.
Weir's answers too.
